Privacy Policy — キーワード調べ

App name: キーワード調べ (Keyword Research). Effective date: November 24, 2026. Home: https://junjungitgit.github.io/

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy explains how the app キーワード調べ ("the app") accesses, uses, stores, shares, and deletes data. The app is a personal tool used only by its operator. It has no other users and is not offered to the public.

2. Data the app accesses

When the operator signs in with Google and grants permission, the app receives:

  • Google OAuth tokens (an access token and a refresh token) for the operator's own Google account, with the Google Ads API scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords).
  • Google Ads API Keyword Planner results for the operator's own Google Ads account: keyword ideas, approximate average monthly searches, and competition levels for keywords the operator enters (names of hotels and products).
  • The operator's own Google Ads customer ID, which is needed to call the API.

The app does not access Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, location, or any other Google user data. It does not collect personal information about anyone other than the operator.

3. How the data is used

  • Keyword ideas and search volumes are used only as hints for choosing topics and wording of the operator's own social media posts.
  • The OAuth tokens are used only to call the Google Ads API Keyword Planner for the operator's own account.
  • The app does not create, change, or serve ads, does not use the data for advertising, and does not use the data to train AI or machine learning models.

4. Sharing

The app does not sell, rent, share, or transfer data received from Google APIs to any third party. No data is disclosed to anyone except as required by law.

5. Storage and protection

  • OAuth tokens and the customer ID are stored only on the operator's own computer, in a local configuration file that is not uploaded to any website, public repository, or shared folder.
  • Keyword results may be stored on the operator's computer and in the operator's own Google Sheets spreadsheet, which only the operator can access.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Keyword results are kept only as long as they are useful for planning posts, and are deleted or overwritten when no longer needed (at most 12 months).
  • OAuth tokens are kept until the operator stops using the app, and are then deleted from the operator's computer.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account "Third-party apps & services" page: https://myaccount.google.com/connections. After revocation, the tokens stop working.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app does not use Google APIs to create, generate, or distribute AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII) or any content that violates the Google APIs Terms of Service.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not collect data from children.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.

10. Contact

Please use the support email address shown on this app's Google OAuth consent screen.
