キーワード調べ

App name: キーワード調べ (Keyword Research) — a personal tool. This page is public and can be read without signing in. There is no sign-in on this website.

What this app does (purpose)

キーワード調べ is a small personal tool used only by its operator. Its purpose is to help the operator decide what to write in posts on the operator's own Threads (social media) accounts about hotels and baby/kids products.

  • The operator signs in with their own Google account and allows the app to access the operator's own Google Ads account.
  • The app calls the Google Ads API Keyword Planner (KeywordPlanIdeaService) to get keyword ideas and approximate monthly search volumes for names of hotels and products (for example, "hotel name + pool" or "hotel name + breakfast").
  • The operator uses these results only as hints about what people are interested in. Facts written in posts are checked on the official hotel or product pages.

What this app does not do

  • It does not create, change, pause, or serve any ads, and it does not spend money.
  • It does not access any Google account, Google Ads account, or data that belongs to anyone other than the operator.
  • It is not offered to the public, and there are no other users.
  • It does not sell, share, or transfer data received from Google APIs.
  • It does not use Google APIs to create, generate, or distribute AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII), sexual content, or any content that violates the Google APIs Terms of Service.
